What is Home Group?


Dallas Tamil Church Home Groups are all about friends, encouragement, prayer and study of God's Word! In a Home Group you can take a break from the busy demands of everyday life and invest in what is really important: your relationship with God and your relationship with the people around you. Home groups will meet for 30 minutes through the Bay Area, once a month.
Get connected in your community!

Why should I go to a Home Group?


Things happen in a small group of people that don't happen in a big group of people. The Bible gives us many examples of how peoples' lives were changed when they met in homes with small groups of people. And the same happens today as we hear of how Home Groups have provided friendship, support, encouragement and an oppurtunity for a closer relationship with Christ. Get connected in a Home Group and your life will change!

Join a Home Group
