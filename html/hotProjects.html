Dallas Tamil Church, TX is a community of Christians from South India living in the Dallas region, TX, USA. We worship, minister and serve the lord in our native language Tamil and are non-denominational. Our mission is to reach the entire Tamil speaking diaspora in the region. The church strives to glorify God in all its endeavors and practices, based on the Bible. Since its inception in Oct 2012, the church has grown into a body of 40 active families (close to 160 members) today. The church operates in two convenient locations, Irving, TX and Plano, TX every Saturday at 6pm and Sunday at 10am CST. The church actively serves in the following areas today;
