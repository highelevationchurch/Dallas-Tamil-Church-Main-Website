WELCOME

We invite you to the Dallas Indian Tamil Church located in Irving and Plano, TX. The church has a unique vision of sharing the gospel of Jesus Christ to the Tamil speaking community in the Dallas county. We share the gospel of Jesus Christ to the people of South Indian Origin who seek to worship the Lord in their own mother tongue (Tamil). As we praise and worship God in this beautiful, rich language, we hope that our friends will know the Lord Jesus and experience the love and power of the Holy Spirit in our church services.

- Pr. Dr. John Henry Mathias and Sis.Christina Henry
