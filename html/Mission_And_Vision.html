MISSION AND VISION

Mission

  1. To do the work that Jesus did.
  2. To build a church that should be the largest hospital bringing health and healing
  3. To build the church where people get saved and receive everlasting life.

Vision

The vision is to do carry the gospel by doing PhD (P=Preach; H=Heal; D=Deliverance). Jesus commanded with these words:
Matthew 10:7-8 And as ye go, preach, saying, The kingdom of heaven is at hand.
Heal the sick, cleanse the lepers, raise the dead, cast out devils: freely ye have received, freely give.

1. Preach

  1. Matthew 4:23 And Jesus went about all Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, and preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing all manner of sickness and all manner of disease among the people.
  2. Luke 4:43 And he said unto them, I must preach the kingdom of God to other cities also: for therefore am I sent.

2. Heal

  1. Matthew 4:24 And his fame went throughout all Syria: and they brought unto him all sick people that were taken with divers diseases and torments, and those which were possessed with devils, and those which were lunatick, and those that had the palsy; and he healed them.

3. Deliver

  1. Luke 4:41 And devils also came out of many, crying out, and saying, Thou art Christ the Son of God. And he rebuking them suffered them not to speak: for they knew that he was Christ.

Timing

1. Sunrise

  1. Wake up before Sunrise as Jesus did before sunrise
    1. Mark 1:35 And in the morning, rising up a great while before day, he went out, and departed into a solitary place, and there prayed.

2. During day

  1. Provide for family by working 8 hours
    1. Psalms 104:23-24 Man goeth forth unto his work and to his labour until the evening.
      O LORD, how manifold are thy works! in wisdom hast thou made them all: the earth is full of thy riches.

3. Sunset

  1. Visit homes and pray as Jesus did after sunset. Jesus healed in Peter's home. Later many came in the evening.
    1. Luke 4:40 Now when the sun was setting, all they that had any sick with divers diseases brought them unto him; and he laid his hands on every one of them, and healed them..
  2. Jesus delivered people in the house.
    1. Luke 4:41 And devils also came out of many, crying out, and saying, Thou art Christ the Son of God. And he rebuking them suffered them not to speak: for they knew that he was Christ.
  3. Jesus preached and did not stay in one place.
    1. Luke 4:43 And he said unto them, I must preach the kingdom of God to other cities also: for therefore am I sent.

Pattern

1. Early church in homes

  1. From Monday until Friday, visit homes after sunset and do PHD (preach, heal, deliver) with those those need help and lead them to Jesus daily.
    1. Acts 5:42 And daily in the temple, and in every house, they ceased not to teach and preach Jesus Christ.

2. Hebrews and church during middle ages, using cathedrals and Basilicas.

  1. Weekly once in the main church to celebrate with testimonies
    1. Hebrews 10:25 Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as the manner of some is; but exhorting one another: and so much the more, as ye see the day approaching.
  2. Come on Sunday and share good report
    1. Luke 10:17-18 And the seventy returned again with joy, saying, Lord, even the devils are subject unto us through thy name.
      And he said unto them, I beheld Satan as lightning fall from heaven.

3. Modern age and international using wide area network called internet.

  1. Internet is the new virtual world connecting minds across boundaries
  2. Low cost and almost everyone has access
  3. Highly scalable and unlimited reach
