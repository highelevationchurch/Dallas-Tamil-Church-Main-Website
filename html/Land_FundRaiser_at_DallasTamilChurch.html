New Elevation - Dallas Tamil Church (NE-DTC), TX, USA is a community of Christians from South India. We worship, minister and serve the lord in our native language Tamil and are non-denominational. Our mission is to reach Indians in the region, plead for God’s favor and evangelize to the local community, with emphasis on the entire Tamil speaking diaspora. The church strives to glorify God in all its endeavors and practices, based on the Bible. Since its inception in Oct 2012, the church has grown into a body of 30 active families (close to 120 members) today. The church operates in two convenient locations, Irving, TX and Plano, TX every Saturday at 6pm and Sunday at 10am CST. The church actively serves in the following areas today;
