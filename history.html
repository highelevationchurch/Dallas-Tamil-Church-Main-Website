HISTORY

On October 13th, 2012, Pr. John & Sis. Christina Henry, along with a small group of around 6 people met at Pastor's home. There, a vision was birthed among His people to start a church for the Tamil-speaking people in the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex. On that day, Dallas Tamil Church was born. Despite the humble beginnings, the Lord blessed the church abundantly and today, the church is growing in Carrollton, TX, and Haslet, TX.

The core vision of the church is to reach out to the lost, empower people with the Word of God, share the Gospel of Jesus Christ and provide an avenue for the Tamil speaking people in the Dallas/Fort Worth metroplex to both worship God and be ministered in their native Tamil language.
